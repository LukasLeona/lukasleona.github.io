PROJECT OVERVIEW

A trip-planning experience built around real traveler needs.

The project explores how a simple itinerary generator can reduce planning friction for Baguio visitors. Instead of presenting a long list of attractions, it organizes the experience around discovery, pacing, and a clearer path from ideas to a usable trip plan.

DIY BAGUIO ITINERARY

Plan one day, a weekend, or a longer Baguio route without unnecessary backtracking.

For a one-day Baguio itinerary, the planner prioritizes a realistic set of nearby stops. For two- or three-day trips, it spreads destinations into manageable daily routes. Longer plans can include more local discoveries while keeping travel time, attraction schedules, and the visitor's preferred pace in view.

Travelers using public transportation can compare walking, jeepney, and taxi guidance, while drivers can organize attractions by area. The result is a flexible Baguio DIY itinerary that can be adjusted before and during the trip.

Trip length1 to 5 days
Travel modesWalk, jeepney, taxi, or drive
Planning focusNearby stops and less backtracking
Useful forSolo, couple, family, and group trips

DESIGN DIRECTION

A quiet visual system inspired by Baguio’s pace.

The interface uses a calm, travel-focused presentation with clear calls to action and lightweight interactions. The goal is to keep attention on the trip itself rather than overwhelm visitors with controls.
