CLEAR, MINIMAL TRACKING

Your visit should remain your business.

This website uses limited event tracking to understand portfolio visits and important button clicks. It is intentionally designed not to collect your IP address, exact location, or a browser fingerprint in messages sent to the site owner.

What the site records

After the portfolio remains visible for a short period, the site may record an engaged visit. It also records when a visitor selects “Explore My Work.” The event can include the page title and path, referral domain, general device category, browser language, and an Asia/Manila timestamp.

Why it is used

The information helps Luke understand whether the portfolio is being discovered and which calls to action are useful. Event notifications are used only for portfolio insights and client-interest signals.

Local storage and notification limits

A timestamp is stored in your browser to prevent repeated alerts from refreshes and multiple clicks. It is not an advertising identifier and is not used to follow you across websites. Visit alerts are limited to once every 24 hours per browser, while button alerts are limited within the current browser session.

Service providers

Email notifications are delivered through EmailJS. Google Analytics can be enabled for aggregate event reporting. These providers may process technical network information under their own privacy policies even though this site does not place that information in Luke’s notification message.

Your choices

The tracker respects supported Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control browser signals. You may also clear this site’s storage through your browser settings. Contact-form details are sent only when you choose to submit the form.

Questions
