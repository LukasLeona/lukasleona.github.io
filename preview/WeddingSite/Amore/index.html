Our story

A quiet hello between the shelves.

Adrian and Camille met on a rainy afternoon in a small independent bookshop. A conversation about the same well-loved novel became coffee, Sunday walks, and a life built from thoughtful little rituals. Years later, Adrian tucked a handwritten proposal into a book made just for Camille—and she turned the page to their forever.

2021

The unexpected hello

They reached for the same novel in a tiny bookshop and stayed to talk until the rain stopped.

2023

A favorite kind of ordinary

Sunday coffee, shared playlists, and spontaneous road trips made everyday life feel extraordinary.

2027

The next page

Inside a handmade book of memories, Camille found one final line: “Will you marry me?”

2028

Forever begins

Now they celebrate the promise of marriage, surrounded by the people they love most.
