How ReadyStation works

From first concept to field-ready thinking.

Each learning sequence follows a consistent cycle designed to build understanding and reinforce safe decision-making.

01 / LEARN

See the essential information.

Clear explanations, key terms, and operational boundaries establish the foundation.

02 / APPLY

Make a decision.

Scenario prompts and sorting activities require the learner to use what they just studied.

03 / VERIFY

Receive useful feedback.

Immediate explanations reinforce correct actions and identify unsafe assumptions.
